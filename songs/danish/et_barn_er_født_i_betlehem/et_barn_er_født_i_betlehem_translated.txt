1. A child is born in Bethlehem, Bethlehem, so let Jerusalem rejoice! 2. A poor virgin sat in labor, sat in labor and gave birth to the Son of the King of Heaven. 3. He was laid in a manger, a manger. God’s angels sang with joy about it: 4. And the wise men from the East offered there, offered there gold, frankincense, and myrrh. 5. All our distress is now gone, all our distress; today a Savior is born to us. 6. We have become God’s beloved children once more, once more; we shall celebrate Christmas in the heavenly city. 7. Upon starry carpets of light blue, light blue, we shall go joyfully to church. 8. May God’s angels teach us quickly, teach us quickly to sing as they sang last night: 9. Then we, like the angels, we, like the angels, shall behold God’s gentle face. 10. To Him be praise for all eternity, for all eternity—to our Savior, our friend, and gentle brother!
