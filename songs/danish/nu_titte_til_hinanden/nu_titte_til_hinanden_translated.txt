1. Now the lovely little flowers gaze at one another, the cheerful birds call to one another; now all the children of the earth open their eyes; now the snail, with its shell on its back, will wander. 2. The dear God and Creator is close to even the smallest worm; He feeds the birds and clothes the lilies of the field, yet He loves the children of humanity most of all; God breathes on the eye when it weeps. 3. God’s Son was Himself a child, and on the straw of the manger He lay; His cradle stood upon the earth, not in the heavens. He has promised the little ones the joy of God’s Kingdom and flowers from the meadows of Paradise. 4. God’s Son loves us so dearly; He is the great friend of children. He carries the child up to God in His arms; He subdued the storm and the sea when He walked on earth, yet the children played at His breast.
