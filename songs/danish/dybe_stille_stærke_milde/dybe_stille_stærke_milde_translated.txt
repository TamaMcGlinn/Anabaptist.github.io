1. Deep, quiet, powerful, gentle words from heaven, gently calling all hearts into the embrace of the Good Shepherd, bearing witness to what has been given to us: Jesus is our way to life! Through Your word, Your grace endures; it is our shield against all dangers. 2. Beloved Savior! We thank You for Your grace toward our earth! Lead us along life’s paths, lead us into the realm of life! Time flows by, the world fades away, yet Your word endures forever. 3. Draw the many fearful souls to You through Your Holy Spirit! May all who face death be blessed by Your mighty saving hand!
