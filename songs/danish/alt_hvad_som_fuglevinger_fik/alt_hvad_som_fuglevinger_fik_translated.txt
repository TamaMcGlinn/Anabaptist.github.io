1. Everything that has bird wings, everything that, following the ways of birds, breathes with the sound of song, praise God for He is good; in His mercy, He provides a remedy for the sorrow and anguish of the dust. 2. My soul, of all things on earth, your thoughts and the words of your tongue have the very best wings, and your breath is freest when you draw it deeply into song, so high in the sky it rings. 3. What good is there on earth that can compare to you in your longing for God’s grace? And it was you whom it sought when, in the most wondrous way, it came to our Lord. 4. So wake up, my soul, break forth with a voice of praise, and praise your God, your Creator and Redeemer, who looked down upon you in mercy and pours out His love upon us through the Comforter!
