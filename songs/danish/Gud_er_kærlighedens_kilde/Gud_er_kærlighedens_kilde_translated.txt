1. God is the boundless source of love, full of peace. He sent His Son to earth; for our sake, He suffered death. I will never be able to forget this great love, and one day I will joyfully praise Him for all eternity. 2. On a cross, victory was won, paid for with Jesus’ blood, and a rich and mighty spring gushed forth with power from the foot of the cross. This river of life flowed from heaven down upon the earth; sin and guilt were met with grace, love, and peace from God. 3. For your love and life, I will thank you every day; you who gave me the gift of faith, grant me the mind of Jesus. You alone I will honor; I will live my life by your word and praise you for the miracle of salvation—that you came to this earth. 4. Draw me into Your thoughts; lead me by Your mighty hand, so that I may be filled with Your grace and strengthened by Your Spirit. Empty me of my own will; let me be completely filled with You, so that Your love, O Lord, may flow through everything within me.
