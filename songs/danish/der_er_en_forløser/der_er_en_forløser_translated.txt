1. There is a Redeemer, the Son of God and Savior; He is Christ; He is worthy, pure, and holy. Thank you, O Father in Heaven, you gave your own Son and left your Spirit on earth until your purpose is fulfilled. 2. Jesus, my Redeemer, you are my friend and Savior. You are Christ, the Lamb of God who atoned for sin and shame. 3. I shall stand before the throne, filled with joy and wonder. I shall see His face and worship Him in all eternity.
