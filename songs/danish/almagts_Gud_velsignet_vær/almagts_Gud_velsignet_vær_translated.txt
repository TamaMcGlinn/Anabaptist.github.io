1. Almighty God, blessed be Your name! We will faithfully praise Your glory, exalt You far and wide, sing along with the hosts of angels, and bow down in the dust before the glory of our King. 2. Lamb of sacrifice with the staff of the cross, blameless and deeply immersed in suffering, you conquered death and the grave by the power that God bestowed upon you; the dominion of hell and the power of death—you have crushed them and laid them in the dust. 3. Let your kingdom extend far and wide, accompanied by peace, now and for all eternity; let every knee on land and on the waves bow for eternal benefit in the name of Jesus. 4. Holy Spirit, to you be praise and glory, together with God the Father and with Jesus, strong and wise, our Bridegroom and dear Brother! May His love for us endure forever and ever!
