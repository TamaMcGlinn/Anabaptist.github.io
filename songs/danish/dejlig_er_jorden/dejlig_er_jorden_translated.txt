1. Lovely is the earth, magnificent is God’s heaven, beautiful is the pilgrimage of souls! Through the fair realms of the earth, we journey to Paradise with song. 2. Times shall come, times shall pass, and generations shall follow in the footsteps of generations. Never will the melody from heaven fall silent in the soul’s joyful pilgrim song. 3. The angels first sang it to the shepherds in the fields, though from soul to soul it resounded: Peace on earth! Rejoice, O man, for an eternal Savior is born to us!
