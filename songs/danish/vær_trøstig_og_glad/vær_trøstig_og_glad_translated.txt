1. Be comforted and rejoice, for God has given us a Savior; go, behold His manger, His cross, and His tomb. Sing praises to Him, give Him glory, He who went to His death; sing praises to Him, say with joy, “He lives again!” 2. Be comforted and rejoice, the Sun of Life has risen; it broke through the clouds, and the darkness vanished. 3. Be comforted and rejoice, Jesus shed His blood; our salvation is complete, our pledge is sure. 4. Be comforted and rejoice, our Redeemer and Friend conquered death and rose again. 5. Be comforted and rejoice, for our King has triumphed; our Advocate now sits on His throne with God. 6. Be comforted and rejoice until the day we are lifted up to meet the Savior with songs of joy.
