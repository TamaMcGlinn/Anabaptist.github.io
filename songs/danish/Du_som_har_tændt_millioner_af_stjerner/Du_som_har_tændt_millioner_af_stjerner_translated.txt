1. You, who have kindled millions of stars, kindle a sparkling faith in our darkness. You are our light, and You watch over and protect us, so that we may sleep in safety and peace. 2. Thank you for the bright day that has passed, the gift you have given us with your own hands. Forgive us for what we did not accomplish; forgive all the wrong we have done or said. 3. Thank you for every joy that filled our hearts, every time you made our lives a celebration. Help us bear every burden, every pain; you alone know what serves us best. 4. Thank you for the people who became our support when we found the path difficult to walk. Help us tomorrow to help the downtrodden; may you yourself meet us in the weak and the small! 5. You, who have kindled millions of stars, will defy the darkness in the world. You are our Father, the one who watches over and protects us, a light in the darkness that comes from us.
