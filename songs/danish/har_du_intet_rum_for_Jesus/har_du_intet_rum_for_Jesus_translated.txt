1. Do you have no room for Jesus, the One who gave His life for you? He is now knocking at your heart, whispering, “Soul, open up for Me!” Make room for Jesus, the King of Glory—oh, open up and invite Him in! He brings salvation with Him; yes, His glory will be yours! 2. Room for pleasure, room for the world, room for sin and wickedness—but no room for Jesus, the One who suffered death for you. 3. Do you have no time for Jesus, the One who wants to set you free? Oh, make use of this time of grace—soon it may be over! 4. Give your heart completely to Jesus; give your thoughts, give your hand; let Him alone rule your life through His good Holy Spirit!
