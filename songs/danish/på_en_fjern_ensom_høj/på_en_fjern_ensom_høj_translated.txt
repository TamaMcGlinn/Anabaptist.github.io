1. On a distant, lonely hill, Jesus’ cross stood somber, a symbol of pain and shame. I love that cross, where God’s Son atoned, when the curse was laid upon Him. Cross, in its power there is victory and song. Let me embrace the holy cross; one day it will be exchanged for a crown. 2. In the ancient cross, stained red with God’s blood, I see a wondrous beauty. It was on that cross that He Himself was rejected; now I no longer fear the judgment. 3. For the ancient cross, my heart is ablaze; I now have my place at its foot; one day it will call me to the land of heaven and to rest with the good Father.
