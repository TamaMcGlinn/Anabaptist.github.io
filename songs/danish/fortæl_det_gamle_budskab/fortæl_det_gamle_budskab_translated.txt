1. Tell me the ancient message—the best I know—about Jesus’ power and grace, about Jesus’ love. Tell it quite simply, as if to a small child; for I am weary and wounded, entangled in the snare of sin. 2. Tell it to me only slowly, so that this message of redemption may sink deep into my soul and mind. Tell it to me often, for I easily forget again; the freshness of the morning dew has faded by noon. 3. Tell it evenly and quietly, yet with solemnity. Remember that I am the sinner whom Jesus wishes to save. Tell it to me always, if truly your voice is to speak comfort to me in every distress and tribulation. 4. Tell me the same message when fear draws near, that the joys of this world have become too dear to me. And when the clarity of the next world dawns with its peace, tell me one last time of Jesus’ love.
