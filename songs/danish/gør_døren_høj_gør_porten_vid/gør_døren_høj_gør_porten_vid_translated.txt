1. Make the door high, make the gate wide! The King of Glory is coming here; He reigns over all the earth and is the Savior of the whole world. 2. He comes down in righteousness, bringing life and salvation; he is gentle and ready to help; his scepter is mercy. 3. He heralds for us the year of joy; through him our distress comes to an end; therefore, a song of joy from countless voices shall greet him. 4. O, blessed indeed is the city whose King is the Lord Christ; and every heart rejoices where this King is welcomed 5. He is the true sun of joy, shining from God’s throne of grace; through Him, the Son of God, light must shine into the dark corners of our hearts. 6. I will open the door of my heart to you, O Jesus; come in to me! Yes, by your grace, let it be that I may see your love! 7. And by your Holy Spirit, guide us on the path to salvation, that here we may sing your praise until we stand before your face!
