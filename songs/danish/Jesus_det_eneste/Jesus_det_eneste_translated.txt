1. Jesus, the only, most holy, purest name ever spoken by human lips. Full of glory, full of love, full of grace, truth, and power. 2. Adversity comes my way, yet You never cast me out of Your holy, mighty embrace. People forget me! Lord, You keep me close to Your heart and call my name. 3. Lord, You hear me; Lord, You guide me—how and where it serves me best! Grant that I may bow down; grant that I may submit to Your will, while I am a guest here! 4. You are the only one, the holiest, the purest; grant me Your pure and holy mind! Deliver me from danger, save me from snares, and finally bring me into Your glory!
