1. Holy, holy, holy! Lord God Almighty! Let our song rise up every morning. Holy, holy, holy, gracious and mighty God on high! Glory be to You! 2. Holy, holy, holy! Behold, around Your throne all the saints kneel, offering praise and joy! Hear, the seraphim’s song of praise is in the same key: God, You are God throughout time and eternity! 3. Holy, holy, holy! Light is your kingdom; sinful eyes are dazzled by all your glory; you alone are holy; none is your equal; none is like you in power and glory. 4. Holy, holy, holy! Lord God Almighty! Let sea, earth, and heaven unite in praise! Holy, holy, holy, merciful and mighty, God on high! Glory be to you!
