1. Do as You will, O God. Whatever happens, You are my Master; I am Your clay. Shape and mold me as You please; I wait quietly, obedient and silent. 2. Do as You will, O God; do as You will with this clay that offers itself to You. Test it and purify it; remove every stone until I am holy, humble, and pure. 3. Do as You will, O God; here at Your feet I lie defeated; my courage is broken; power and strength belong to You alone, O Lord; touch me, heal me now! 4. Do as You will, God; let Your banner wave over me: “Holy are You.” Fill me with the Spirit; inscribe this motto: “Christ alone, always, is my life!”
