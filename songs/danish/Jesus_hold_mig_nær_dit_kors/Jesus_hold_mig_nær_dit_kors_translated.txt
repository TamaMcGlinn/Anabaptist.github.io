1. Jesus, keep me close to Your cross, where a glorious spring, free for all, full of healing, flows year after year. Jesus’ cross, Jesus’ cross is my praise and glory; there my glorified soul shall wear the crown. 2. Near the cross, grace, filled with repentance, raised me up; the radiance of the morning star clothed me in its light. 3. Near the cross! Holy Spirit, make this clear to me; let me walk day by day safely in its shadow! 4. Near the cross I wait, watch, hope, and rest, until, rejoicing at Jesus’ command, I hasten home to heaven.
