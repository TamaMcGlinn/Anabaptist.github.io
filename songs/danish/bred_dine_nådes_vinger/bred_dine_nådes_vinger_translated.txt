1. Spread the wings of Your mercy over me, my Jesus, and let me rest peacefully with You in good times and bad. Be my strength, my joy, my wisdom, and my comfort, and let me follow You faithfully and gladly every day. 2. Forgive me my sins, and cleanse me in Your blood; give me a humble heart and a new and good will. Keep us all—great and small—close to Your saving heart; let us once again go to rest for the night in Your peace.
