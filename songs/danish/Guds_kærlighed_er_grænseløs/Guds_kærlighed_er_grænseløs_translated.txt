1. God’s love is boundless, so rich, so deep, and so mighty. He sent us His own Son for salvation and atonement. Behold the pain Jesus bore when God left Him alone, but through suffering and death He won a victory for all eternity. 2. Arise and look upon the Savior, who bore our sin on the cross while my voice rang out in mockery and I scoffed along with the others. It was for me that He sacrificed Himself; for me He had to suffer. His struggle with death gave me life; His victory became my salvation. 3. I can take no credit for anything, for all I received by grace—my life, my all—belongs to Him who triumphed over death. Why He loved me so deeply, I can never fully comprehend. But one thing I know: His love has saved my soul for eternity.
