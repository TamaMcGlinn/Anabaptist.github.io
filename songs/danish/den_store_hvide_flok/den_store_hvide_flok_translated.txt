1. The great white multitude we see like a thousand snow-capped mountains, surrounded by forests of palm branches—before the throne, where are they? It is the host of heroes who emerged from that great tribulation and have found their way through the blood of the Lamb into the sanctuary of Heaven! There they now worship with ceaseless sounds of joy in lofty choirs, where God dwells amid the singing of all the angels. Now they dwell where life is best, celebrating an eternal feast in the leafy hall with Him, and the Lamb Himself, by the river of life, is both host and guest. 2. Here they walked amid great contempt, but behold them now, standing before the throne in all their splendor, crowned and clothed in the priestly robes of Heaven! Lift up your voice, wave palm branches, and sing of Heaven’s power and might: Eternal praise be to you, our God and the Lamb! It is true that in so much distress, streams of tears often flowed down their cheeks, but God, as soon as they came home, wiped them away on His lap, 3. Congratulations, dear friend, yes, oh, a thousandfold congratulations, then, that you were here so faithful above all and departed from here so well! You have spurned the world’s comfort; so live now in eternal bliss, and reap what you have sown with sighs and tears amid the joy of a thousand angels!
