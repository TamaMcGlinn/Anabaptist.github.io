1. God, You send us Your love; in the midst of darkness, You kindle Your light. Jesus, come—let Your light shine upon us; set us free, so that we may find Your peace. Give us Your light, give us Your light. Give us your light; let your glory fill the land. Come, Holy Spirit; kindle your fire within us. Come like a stream, like a river full of peace and grace. Give us Your word, Jesus, give us Your light. 2. God, we honor Your holy name; we desire to be in Your light. You have given us Your blood and Your life, and from death You brought us to life. Give us Your light, give us Your light. 3. When we seek to walk in the light, Your love will change everything. Through our faith, our lives, and our walk, we will reflect Your light in the midst of the world. Give us Your light, give us Your light.
