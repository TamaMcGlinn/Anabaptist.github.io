1. What a friend we have in Jesus! He knows all things and can do all things. He lightens even our heaviest burdens when we turn to Him in prayer. Alas, but often our peace is lost, and sorrow and toil become our reward, simply because we do not bring everything before God in prayer. 2. When we face trials and struggles, and life’s battle seems hard, we must never lose heart if we turn to God in prayer. Even if all other friends fail us, Jesus is with us in return; He hears our sighs and shares our sorrow when we turn to Him in prayer. 3. If we are weak, if we are afraid, if our sin stands before our eyes, Jesus is our mighty Savior; we dare to go to Him in prayer. Whether the sun shines bright and warm, or thunder roars, bring everything—in joy as in sorrow—before God in prayer.
