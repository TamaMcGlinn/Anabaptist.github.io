1. Praise God with songs of joy in His sanctuary. Praise His gospel amid sounds of jubilation. Let all that breathes praise the Lord; sing to our God a song pure and clear, openly expressing His love; sing His praise—Alleluia. 2. Praise God with stringed instruments; let the sound of the harp be heard. Sing songs of thanksgiving; praise Him with joy. 3. Praise God with holy songs, melodious and clear. Praise Him with festive sounds, all who have breath. 4. Praise the Lord on earth, among the people of God. Join the heavenly choir of angels in praising God’s exalted majesty.
