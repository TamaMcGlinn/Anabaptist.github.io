1. Praise God with songs of joy in His sanctuary. Praise His gospel amid the sound of jubilation. Let all who breathe praise the Lord; sing to our God a pure and clear song for His love now openly revealed; sing His praise—Hallelujah. 2. Praise God with stringed instruments; let the sound of the harp be heard. Sing songs of thanksgiving; praise Him with joy. 3. Praise God with holy songs, melodious and clear. Let all who breathe praise Him with festive sounds. 4. Praise the Lord steadily on earth, among the people set by God; with the heavenly choir of angels, praise God’s lofty majesty.
