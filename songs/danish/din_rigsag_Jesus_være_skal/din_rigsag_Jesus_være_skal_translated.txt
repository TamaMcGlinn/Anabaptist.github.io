1. Your kingdom, Jesus, shall be my greatest glory. Thank You that I, too, received Your call to be here with You! I am unworthy, dear Lord, to belong to the army of the Kingdom, but You, who gave me the name of a friend and lovingly took me into Your embrace, grant me the strength to go where You have told me to go! Help me to see with Your eyes every people to whom life and boundaries were given, and to bear the world’s distress and shame with my gaze turned toward You, the Lamb of God, faithful unto death, patient, strong, and joyful! 2. Give me, Jesus, your saving spirit for the sorrow and grief of my people. God’s kingdom stands in radiant splendor; Christ has gathered his precious bride from every tribe, from every land, each bearing a victor’s wreath as a sign; in a chorus of joy they praise the great Lord. Enfold me in Your suffering and make me strong and warm 3. Then I know for certain that my eyes will behold a glorious sight, When the world draws toward its evening, when joy and sorrow have come to an end.
