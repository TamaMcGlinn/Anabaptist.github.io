1. Fill my lamp with oil, O Lord; set my heart ablaze for You. Fill my lamp with oil; I pray, let me burn as a light for You. Sing to the Lord, Hallelujah; sing to the Lord; sing out your joy; sing to the Lord, Hallelujah; sing with joy to your God. 2. Fill my heart with joy, O Lord; let me sing with delight for You. Fill my heart with joy; I pray, let me give thanks and praise to You. 3. Fill my heart with peace, dear Lord; let me rest in faith in You. Fill my heart with peace, God, I pray; there is rest, there is peace in You. 4. Fill my heart with love, Lord; let me devote my life to You. Fill my heart—I pray, O Lord—let me serve You, God, You alone.
