1. As the firm foundation of salvation, I know only the blood and love of Jesus; neither feelings nor the struggles of the flesh can make me worthy before my God. Every other foundation is treacherous sand; every other foundation is treacherous sand! 2. Though doubt has cast me into darkness, I know that grace remains in power. Despite storms and despite the tossing waves, the anchor of my faith holds fast. 3. His oath and covenant, His death and blood shall carry me through the river of fear. When all things falter and pass away, in Him my hope and comfort remain. 4. He will soon come in power and glory; may I then be one of His own, and, clothed in His righteousness, go home to eternal joy and peace.
