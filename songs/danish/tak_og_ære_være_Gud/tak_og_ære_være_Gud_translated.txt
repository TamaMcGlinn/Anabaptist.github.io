1. Thanks and glory be to God, who has fulfilled His promise and sent His beloved Son down to earth for the salvation of sinners. 2. What the ancient fathers so earnestly desired, and what was promised of old, the Lord has gloriously fulfilled. 3. Welcome, O my peace; to you be thanks forever! Come, O Jesus, into my heart; make your own way here! 4. Come, O King of Glory, come! My heart is yours; purify it and shape it as you will; I belong only to you. 5. Fill my soul with Your comfort because of my fear and poverty, that in faith I may cling to You always. 6. So that when You come down here a second time in glory, I may go to meet You and stand righteous before You.
