1. Go, Zion, to meet your King; Jerusalem, rejoice in God! Scatter palm branches with joy for the Messiah, and be like the bride who waits! Rejoice, rejoice! Yes, rejoice in the Lord your God! Rejoice, rejoice, and sing to your King and God 2. He comes from eternal joys, leaving His throne of crystal, His glory in the palaces of light, and lies on straw in a stable. 3. He comes and offers a sacrifice for you on the bloody tree; He dies for our sinful world, bringing righteousness with Him. 4. He comes from the grave with the spoils, and sunlight is kindled in the night. For the dead who slumber in the grave, the time of silence has ended. 5. He comes to the grieving heart and raises up what had fallen. He comes in power and shall reign until God is all in all.
