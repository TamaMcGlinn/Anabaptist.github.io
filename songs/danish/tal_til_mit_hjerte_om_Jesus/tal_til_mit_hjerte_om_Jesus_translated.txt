1. Speak to my heart about Jesus; let every word sink deep into my soul; let me hear anew the most wonderful message on earth! Remind me of the angels’ rejoicing long ago in the fields of Bethlehem: “Glory to God in the highest! Peace on earth!” That is how it rang out. Speak to my heart about Jesus; let every word sink deep into my soul; let me hear anew the most wonderful message on earth! 2. Speak of that mighty encounter, when He faced our mortal enemy, drawing as His victorious weapon the sword of the Word of Life. Remind me of the Savior’s toil, tears, and bloody sweat, while only blessings for others followed His blessed path. 3. Speak of His departure on the cross, the Savior’s deathbed so harsh; sing of that morning in the garden, eternity’s lovely spring! Sing of the miracle of redemption, the mighty power of love; as I listen with tears, I rejoice: It is finished.
