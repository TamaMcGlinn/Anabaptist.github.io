1. The scent of light-green grass wafts from ditches, fields, and meadows. And the wind kisses the dunes and headlands and ruffles the beds of wildflowers. God’s sun penetrates body and mind, heralding the coming of a warm and bright midsummer. 2. Hear the thousand-voiced chorus of birds from morning till night! They vie to sing a song that praises the Creator’s power; every seed and creature in the earth’s depths feels a joy of life within as high as the skylarks. 3. Behold the splendor of the blossoms’ colors—it puts all words to shame. King Solomon in his coronation robes must pale with envy! All that is beautiful, all love—even the smallest bird and lily—is, O God, Your creative will. 4. Yes, You make all things new on earth—a summer rich in grace. Yet Your Word shines most clearly through the mystery of love. All flesh must die; every flower withers. When the blossoms stand withered and dry, the Word blooms eternally. 5. Yes, Jesus Christ is the Word that brings life from the dead; so the heavens are renewed, the earth is renewed—a world green with growth. Come soon, Jesus, and make it clear that the morning is coming when an eternal summer dawns.
