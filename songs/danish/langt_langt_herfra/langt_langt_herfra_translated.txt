1. Far, far from here, near the town of Bethlehem, angels sang so joyfully to the shepherds. Glory to God! Glory to God! Glory to God in the highest, peace on earth; God’s favor has come to people today. 2. How glorious was the song of love, the message of mercy sent down from heaven. 3. Hasten the time when, in every land, the song will ring out with true joy.
