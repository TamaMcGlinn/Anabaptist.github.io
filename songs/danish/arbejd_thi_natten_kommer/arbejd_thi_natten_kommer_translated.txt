1. Work, for the night is coming; work from dawn; work when the light of the east promises a new day. Work while the day unfolds beneath the sun’s heat; work, for night is coming, when no work can be done. 2. Work, for night is coming; attend to your calling with diligence. Fill the day’s embrace with busyness; the time for rest is near. Make every minute count before it slips away; work, for night is coming—the day is but short. 3. Work, for night is coming; the western skies glow golden; there is still much to do—work, for the light is fading. Work until the last ray of sunlight fades from the day’s path; work while darkness falls—for then all is over.
