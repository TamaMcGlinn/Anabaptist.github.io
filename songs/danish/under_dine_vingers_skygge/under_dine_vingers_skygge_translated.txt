1. Under the shadow of Your wings, Lord, let me dwell and build, until You call me home, until my weary eyes close, until, at the hour of death, I sigh: “Lord, receive my spirit!” Lord, take my spirit to You! 2. Under the shadow of Your wings—there, and there alone, is the joy of life, the Spirit’s nursery on earth, where what we lost of Paradise through Adam’s fall once again sprouts and grows. Springs forth and grows once more. 3. Under the shadow of Your wings, as in the safe haven of our childhood home, we walk undaunted through storm and sleet; though we are not spared from danger, You preserve us from the power and snare of darkness, our God. You preserve us, our God. 4. Under the shadow of Your wings, let only the burden of the cross weigh upon us, and let lamentations sound as we walk! Father! Your hands of wisdom turn the cross into the palm of victory, and cries of lament into songs of joy. Cries of lament into songs of joy. 5. Under the shadow of Your wings, let the temple of my heart be adorned with my faith, my love, light and purity, strength and warmth, so that at last, in Your arms, I may slumber in peace! I may slumber in peace!
