1. There is a gate that stands open, through which, like the dawn, a radiant glow streams from the cross upon which our Savior died. Open up for me! For me, for me—it has been opened for me! 2. Through this gate, anyone who desires salvation may enter freely, and whoever prays will receive God’s grace every day. 3. Press forward so that you may enter while the gate of grace stands open; take up the cross and win the crown with the mighty weapon of faith. 4. By the river’s waters we lay down the cross we must bear here; in its place, we are adorned with the crown of life to God’s glory.
