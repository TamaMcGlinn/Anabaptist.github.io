1."Christ the Lord is ris'n today," Sons of men and angels say, Raise your joys and triumphs high, heav'ns and earth reply. 2.Lives again our glorious King, Where, O death, is now thy sting? Dying once He all doth save, victory, O grave? 3.Love's redeeming work is done, Fought the fight, the battle won, Death in vain forbids His rise: opened paradise! 4.Soar we now, where Christ has led? Foll'wing our exalted Head, Made like Him, like Him we rise, cross, the grave, the skies!
