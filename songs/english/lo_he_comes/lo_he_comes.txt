1.Lo! He comes, with clouds descending, Once for favored sinners slain; Thousand, thousand saints attending Swell the triumph of His train; Hallelujah! Hallelujah! Hallelujah! Christ appears in pow'r to reign. 2.Ev'ry eye shall now behold Him, Robed in dreadful majesty; Those who set at naught and sold Him, Pierced, and nailed Him to the tree, Deeply wailing, Deeply wailing, Deeply wailing, Shall the true Messiah see. 3.Now redemption, long expected See in solemn pomp appear; All His saints, by man rejected, Now shall meet Him in the air. Hallelujah! Hallelujah! Hallelujah! See the day of God appear. 4.Yea, Amen! Let all adore Thee, High on Thine eternal throne; Saviour, take the pow'r and glory. Claim the kingdom for Thine own; Hallelujah! Hallelujah! Hallelujah! O come ly; come, Lord, come.
