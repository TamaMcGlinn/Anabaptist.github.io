1.Guide me, O Great Jehovah! Pilgrim through this barren land; I am weak, but Thou art mighty, Hold me with Thy pow'rful hand: Bread heaven! Bread heaven! Feed more, Feed now and evermore. 2.Open Thou crystal fountain, Whence the healing stream doth flow: Let the fiery, cloudy pillar Lead me all my journey through: Strong liv'rer! Strong liv'rer! Be shield, Be still my strength and shield. 3.If I tread verge of Jordan, Bid my anxious fears subside: Bear me through the swelling torrent, Land me safe on Cannaan's side: Songs praises! Songs praises! I Thee, I ever give to Thee. 4.Saviour, come! long to see Thee, Long to dwell with Thee above; And to know in full communion, All the sweetness of Thy love. Come, Jesus! Come, Jesus! Take home, Take waiting people home.
