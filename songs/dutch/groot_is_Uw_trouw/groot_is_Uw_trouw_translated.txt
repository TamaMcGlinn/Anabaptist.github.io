Great is Your faithfulness, O Lord, my God and Father. There is no shadow of change in You. Though I am unfaithful, You remain forever the same as You have always been, and You prove this even now. Great is Your faithfulness, O Lord; great is Your faithfulness, O Lord, shown to me anew every morning. You have given me everything I needed; great is Your faithfulness, O Lord, shown to me. Summer and winter, spring and harvest, the sun, moon, and stars, set in their courses—all of Your creation testifies to Your faithfulness, Lord, who preserves and guides us throughout our lives. You give us peace, forgiveness of sins, and Your presence, which strengthens and guides us; strength for today, joyful hope for the future—You give life for all eternity.
