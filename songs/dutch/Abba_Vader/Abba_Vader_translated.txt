Abba, Father, You alone—I belong to You. You alone know my heart; it belongs to You. May my heart always be fervent; You never leave me alone. Abba, Father, You alone—I belong to You. Abba, Father, let me belong only to You. May my will forever be Yours and no other’s. Let my heart never be cold, Lord. Let me never leave You. Abba, Father, let me belong only to You.
