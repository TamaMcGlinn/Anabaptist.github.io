Where people wander in the dark, turn around and see the new light—see the light that God gave us in Jesus, see the One who enlightens every person. For the light is stronger than the darkness, and daylight overcomes the night; do not search for your way in the darkness any longer—turn around and see God’s new day. Where people suffer injustice in a world that finds no peace, have faith; bear the cross with joy—there is light that overcomes all things. Light a candle against all the darkness—as a sign in a time of fear—that our lives do not end in despair; that peace is stronger than strife.
