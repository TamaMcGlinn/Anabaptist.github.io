1. How beautiful the world will be on that day, when Jesus returns on the clouds, when all living things—the nations and peoples—will bow before Him in awe. 2. How beautiful the world will be on that day, when God will pitch His tent among the people, when nations, no longer separated by borders, will gather under Christ’s banner. 3. How beautiful the world will be on that day, when all of creation will be purified, when the earth will be renewed and healed of all our evil, of all our wrongdoing. 4. How beautiful the world will be on that day, when God will restore what we have ruined, when all who are deeply weary will rest, when tears are replaced by laughter. 5. How beautiful the world will be on that day, when God will crown His people with glory and dwell in the New Jerusalem—how beautiful the world will be on that day. How beautiful the world will be on that day.
