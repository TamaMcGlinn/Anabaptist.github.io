Whether joy is my lot, or sorrow consumes me, and storm winds and night terrify me. You, my Savior, have taught me to praise You: it is good whatever my Lord ordains for me. It is good what God does: it is good whatever my Lord ordains for me. Your lot—how much heavier it was here! How Satan set his snare! And looking upon Your cross, my spirit also praises You; Lord, let Your comfort be near me in my dying, that Your love may then refresh me; Then I give thanks, no matter how fearful my agony may be;
