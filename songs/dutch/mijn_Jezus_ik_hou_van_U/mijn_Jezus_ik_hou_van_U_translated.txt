My Jesus, I love You; I call You my friend. For You took upon Yourself the punishment I deserved. You are the great Redeemer, my Savior; I have loved You, but never as much as I do now. My Jesus, I love You, for You loved me. When You hung on the cross, a wound in Your side. Grace for me, a crown of thorns for You; I will love You in life and in death. And I will praise You, even in my time of need. When I am about to die, I will call out to You; When I enter Your glory, Your eternity, I will bow before You in Your sanctuary.
