Jesus, Redeemer of my soul, You are worthy that I kneel before You. God’s long-awaited promise was fulfilled in glory. Jesus, Savior, to You be the glory; You alone are our Lord. Lamb of God, slain for us, to You be praise and thanks. You have fulfilled Your purpose on the cross; all God’s children rejoice together. Jesus, Redeemer of my soul, You are worthy that I kneel before You. Rock of the ages, comfort in sorrow, let me take refuge in Your heart, until the trumpet sounds again, and the choir of the blessed sings. You are worthy that I kneel before You, Jesus, Redeemer of my soul.
