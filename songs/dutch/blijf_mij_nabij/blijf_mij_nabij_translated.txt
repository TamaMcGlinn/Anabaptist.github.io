Stay close to me when darkness falls. Night descends, and no light shines anymore. Other helpers, Lord, have forsaken me. Help of the helpless, be near me. Be with me now that the day is drawing to a close. Everything that once possessed radiance and glow fades away. Everything fades in the shifting tides, but You who are eternal, stay close to me. I need You; Your grace is my only light in night and darkness. Who else will be my guide but You? In night and adversity, Lord, stay close to me. I fear no evil, for the Lord is with me. Tears and sorrow are no longer bitter. Where is your sting, O death? What threat do you pose to me? I triumph; the Lord is near to me. Hold, O Lord, Your cross high before my failing eyes, a light in the darkness, showing the way upward. Your day is dawning; the shadow is passing. In death and in life, Lord, be near to me.
