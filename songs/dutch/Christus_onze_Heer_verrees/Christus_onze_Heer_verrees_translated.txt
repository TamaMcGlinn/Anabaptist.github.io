Christ, our Lord, has risen, hallelujah! A holy day after fear and dread, hallelujah! He who died on the cross, hallelujah, brought us home into God’s freedom, hallelujah! Now let us praise Christ in our song, who reigns in glory, Who accepted the cross and the tomb, that He might give life to sinners, But His suffering and His struggle have prepared atonement for us; Now He is the Lord of heaven, and angels rejoice in His honor,
