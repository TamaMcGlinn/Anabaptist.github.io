I see the land where the sun never sets and the angels, in shining robes, bathed in God’s love and His light, where the Lamb stands as the sacrifice for me. The path of His children leads to that land full of light. In that land of God’s salvation, we shall stand before God’s throne, rejoicing. In that land, we shall sing forever the glorious song of redemption and peace; in that land, where no sigh is heard, we are free from oppression and sorrow. In that land there is no sin or sorrow, no temptation or malice in the heart; sickness and pain are unknown there—oh, how wonderful it will be there! In that land there is no hunger or thirst; the fountain of life overflows; worries are no longer borne there; Christ will be all in all. To the Father, so gentle and so good, our song of praise rises in a tone of gratitude; yes, we praise Him for Jesus’ blood, for the salvation wrought for us by God’s Son.
