Take my life, Lord, and let it be dedicated to Your glory. Make my hours and my time ready for Your praise and service. Take my hands, and make them strong, faithful, and skilled for Your work. Let me set my feet on the paths of Your law. Take my voice, so that my song may pay homage to You, my King. Make, O Lord, my lips pure, that they may be Your witnesses. Take my silver and my gold, that I may withhold nothing from You. Make my strength and my mind instruments in Your hand. Take my sins and my guilt into the care of Your patience. Grant that I, a wayward child, may always find my way back to You. Take also my love, Lord; I lay its treasures before You. Take me, and I am devoted to You forever.
