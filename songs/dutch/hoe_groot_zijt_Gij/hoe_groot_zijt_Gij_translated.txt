O great God, when I see in this world the beauty of what You have made, when I behold how everything that has once been touched by Your hand glorifies You, then my heart rejoices toward You, glad and joyful: How great You are! How great You are! Then my heart rejoices toward You, glad and joyful: How great You are! How great You are! I see how Jesus walked on earth, filled with love for a sinful people; I see how He accepted the cup of suffering and atoned for the debt of sin on the cross. When God calls me, when I must die, when I may behold His glory, when I may inherit the Kingdom and stand before His throne on the Last Day.
