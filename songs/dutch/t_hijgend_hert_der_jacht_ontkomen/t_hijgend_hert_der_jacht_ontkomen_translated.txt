The panting deer, having escaped the hunt, does not cry out more earnestly for the delight of fresh streams of water than my soul longs for God. Yes, my soul thirsts for the Lord: God of life! Oh, when will I draw near before Your eyes, to exalt Your name in Your house? I have made my tears my food day and night as I lament, for my mockers dare to ask, “Where is God, whom you await?” My distressed soul melts away when it recalls how I once rejoiced with God’s joyful hosts to the sound of voices and strings. O my soul, why do you bow down? Why are you troubled? Nourish your old trust once more; find your delight in the praise of the Most High, for God’s goodness will one day turn your distress into joy. Hope in God, lift your eyes upward, for I will still praise His name. But the Lord will provide a way out—He who bestows His favor each day. I will live in this trust and proclaim it in my song; I will sing His praise even in the night, as I wait for Him and lift my heart—whatever may befall me—to the God of my life.
