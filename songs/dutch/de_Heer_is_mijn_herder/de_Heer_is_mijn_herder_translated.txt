The Lord is my Shepherd! I have all I need; He will lead me to green pastures. He gently leads me beside still waters. He watches over my soul; He leads me in paths of goodness and blessing; He sustains me when I falter; He lifts me up when I fall. Even though the grave may threaten, I will fear no evil; You will be with me; O Lord, Your rod and Your staff comfort me! In the heart of the desert, His heavenly gifts refresh and revive me; He will strengthen me with bread and wine. To Him I remain devoted! I will dwell forever in the house of my Lord: thus His love will always crown me with its blessing.
