1. Will there ever be a day of peace, will there ever be liberation for those who are silenced, whose lives are broken forever? 2. Will there ever be a lasting present filled with true peace, where pain is no longer suffered and life will be renewed? 3. Behold the branches on the trees, where young green buds are sprouting, leading to a radiant new summer where the scent of peace blossoms. 4. Behold the stars in the sky, where the darkness of the night is dispelled by their radiance, giving way to a new day that smiles. 5. Just as trees show people that there is power to grow, so will the Son of Man come, who is the Tree of Life. 6. Just as stars tell people that no night is too dark, so will a child come to save us, who is the Light of the World.
