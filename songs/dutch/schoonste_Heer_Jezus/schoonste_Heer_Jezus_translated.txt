Most Beautiful Lord Jesus, Lord of all realms, Son of God, Son of Mary. I want to love You, I want to honor You; You are my soul’s joy and crown. Beautiful are the meadows, beautiful are the forests, in the beautiful springtime. Jesus is more beautiful, Jesus is purer, He who gladdens our sorrowful hearts. Beautiful is the moonlight, more beautiful is the sunlight, and all the stars; Jesus shines more beautifully, Jesus shines more purely, than the host of angels in heaven’s hall. Beautiful are the flowers, more beautiful are the people, in the prime of their lives. They must die, and one day perish, but Jesus lives forever.
