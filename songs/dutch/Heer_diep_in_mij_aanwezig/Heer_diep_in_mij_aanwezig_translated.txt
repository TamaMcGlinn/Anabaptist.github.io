Lord, present deep within me, You know me through and through. Never am I doing anything without You already knowing why. While my lips wait as I search for words, You read all my thoughts—I am an open book. Where can I flee from You? Not into a dark valley. Not far beyond the heavens, deep in the vast universe. Even if I were to travel all the paths on the wings of the wind, I would still encounter You—I know that You will find me. No night can hide me; You see right through it, as if a new morning were dawning at midnight. Even before I was born, You knew of my existence; You planned long ago the path I would take. God who can fathom me, penetrate my very being; see if I truly belong to what is right. And whether I wander down winding paths or keep to the straight and narrow, let me be guided by the path You show me.
