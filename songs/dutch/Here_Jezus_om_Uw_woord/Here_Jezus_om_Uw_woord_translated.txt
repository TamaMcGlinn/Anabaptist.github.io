Lord Jesus, we have gathered here because of Your word. Let Your grace flow into the hearts that listen to You. Sanctify us, that we may give You our hearts, our souls, and our whole lives. Our feelings and our minds, O Lord, are so lacking in clarity; unless Your Spirit dispels the night and sets us in the light of truth. You Yourself must work within us to think, act, and speak what is good. O You, splendor of glory, light from light, born of God, prepare us for Your salvation; open our hearts, mouths, and ears, so that our prayers and our songs may reach heaven.
