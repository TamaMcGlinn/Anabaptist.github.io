Joy or gladness, sorrow or grief? There is a God. There is a God. Pour out your heart to Him, O human being. There is a God who hears. Always turn to Him for help and counsel. Do not wait too long; soon it will be too late. Let not doubt disturb your heart. There is a God who hears. God gave His Son in a stable in Bethlehem. Lord of the Universe. Lord of the Universe. He endured humiliation and countless insults. Ruler of the entire universe. For our Creator, King of the earth, did not even spare His own Son. Go to Him, then, as the morning light dawns. He is the God who hears. From east to west, from south to north. O man, spread the word. People, spread the word. May the Lord’s love be heard far and wide. Children of men, spread the word! Do not wander in darkness; do not go alone. But seek salvation in Jesus alone. May all your hope be fixed on Him: Jesus is the eternal Light!
