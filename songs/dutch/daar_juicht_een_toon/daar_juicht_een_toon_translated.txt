There a melody resounds, there a voice rings out, echoing throughout all Jerusalem. A glorious morning light is breaking; the Son of God has risen. No tomb could hold the Son of David; He conquered—that mighty Hero! He rose from the tomb by the Father’s power, for He is God, clothed in might. Now death no longer instills fear, for everything—everything—is fulfilled for those who look to Jesus in faith, who do not fear death or hell. For now that the Lord has risen, now new life begins. A life prepared through His death, a life in His glory.
