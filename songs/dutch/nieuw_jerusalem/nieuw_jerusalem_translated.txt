City of Light with Your pearly gates, wondrous city built so high—never has anyone on this earth beheld Your glory. There I will meet my Lord, listen to His voice of love; there will be no more mourning or tears in the New Jerusalem. Holy place full of light and glory, where the tree of life blossoms and the river of living water flows through the golden City of God. What joy it will be to be united with Him soon. In that city with pearly gates, in the New Jerusalem.
