Tell me about Jesus, my Savior—I love to listen to His word! Sing to me the song of salvation—never has a more beautiful song been heard! Tell me how the jubilant choirs sang joyfully through the night, When He was born for us, the One who brought us salvation. Tell me about Jesus, my Savior—I love to listen to His word. Nothing on earth has ever so charmed my heart. Tell me how Jesus, my Savior, endured hunger and pain; how the tempter tormented Him in the desolate wilderness. Let me hear of His love; tell me of His suffering for me. He was despised and rejected, poor and forsaken for my sake. Tell me of the cross of shame, where He once gave His life; how, in triumph and glory, Jesus rose from the grave. Great was His love for sinners—who has ever loved us so? Tell me again of my Savior; He is my dearest friend. One day my Savior will come again, come as King of the earth! Then angels will rejoice once more, gathered around the Savior; Will we, too, with the blessed, be with the Lord forever?
