He who calmly and quietly submits himself to God’s will, trusts and believes in Him in all things, listens only to His voice, and gives himself entirely to Him, tastes a joy that never fades. Look only to Him, follow His voice obediently; just keep trusting calmly. Always keep your eyes on Him. When the furnace of life roars, when worries come our way: it is His smile that dispels everything. Complaints, sighs, weeping, fear, and doubt fade away when we trust in Him with calm confidence. If people bring you hardship and strife: look to Him; His gaze alone gives us courage! He carries you, and your cross as well, will soon bring you safely home, and ensures that everything here works together for good.” Remain faithful until death; whether in prosperity or distress, ensure that the fire within you never goes out! What He says must be done; where He sends you, you must go—no objections; trust and believe. Oh, how great is the joy when one walks with God, goes through life here honestly and sincerely, whether in sorrow or joy, rejoicing in Him, and having laid everything on the altar.
