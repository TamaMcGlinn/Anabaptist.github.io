You, faithful Shepherd, You give strength to all who wait on You in prayer. You lead Your children by Your hand to the land You have promised. Your gentle hand, full of tenderness, protects Your sheep from straying. You wish to water them with Your blood; You quench their thirst in abundance. How can I give thanks in this song for all that You offer to humanity? What You gave us is undeserved: grace in place of deserved punishment.
