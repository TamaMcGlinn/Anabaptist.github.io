I feel God’s winds today; today I hoist the sail. It is battered and heavy with foam, but I hoist it in hope of salvation. For Christ Himself, as a silent guest, travels with me in my little boat. At His command, I dare to set out onto the wild, high seas! The tears I have wept have been dried by God’s wind. I no longer think of what I once tried in vain. But with renewed courage, I make a firm decision: I feel God’s winds today and sail out of the harbor! O, let me never forget, Lord, how You show Your love. Help me remember how You were cruelly crowned with thorns. And, wise Pilot, as I take the risk and hoist my sails once more—now that I feel God’s winds today—You guide me on my journey!
