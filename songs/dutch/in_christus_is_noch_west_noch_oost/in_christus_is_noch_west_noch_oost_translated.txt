In Christ there is neither west nor east, neither south nor north; through His comfort, humanity is made one; through His word, the world is made one. Every heart that belongs to Him finds fellowship with Him. Service to Him is the golden cord that binds us all together. Beloved, join together, wherever you are from and whoever you may be; gather around your Father like children, devoted to Christ. Let the south and the north now rejoice; let the west and the east praise Him. The whole world belongs to Christ; in Him it finds comfort.
