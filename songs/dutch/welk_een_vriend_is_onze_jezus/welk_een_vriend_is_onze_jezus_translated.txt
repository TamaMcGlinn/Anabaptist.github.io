1. What a friend is our Jesus, Who is willing to take our place! What a privilege that through Him, I may always come freely to God. Often we lose much peace; often sin weighs us down—precisely because we do not bring it all to our Lord in prayer. 2. If the path sometimes leads through temptation, causing our hearts to tremble in the hour of struggle, do we then bring all our struggles to Him who grants salvation? Can a friend ever be more faithful than He who bears our suffering? Jesus offers us healing; He alone is the One who sustains us. 3. Are we weak, burdened, weighed down, and oppressed by worry? Dear Savior, our Refuge, You are our Help and Surety! When friends sometimes forsake us, let us go to the Lord in prayer. In His arms we are safe; He will never forsake us.
