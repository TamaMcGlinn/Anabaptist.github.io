I worship You, O power of love, revealed in Jesus. God sought me too, no matter how much I grieved Him; His Son came to earth for me as well. I forget myself when I am overwhelmed by the waves of such love. How deeply do You love me, and how does Your heart long for me? You meet me with Your love, so that I, too, may be Yours. You, faithful Lord, have chosen me: I want to belong to You forever. I want to seek You, to give myself to You; only in You do I find refreshment. In You alone do I have life, and not in anything on earth. With You is rest, with You is joy; to You, Lord, my heart wants to devote itself.
