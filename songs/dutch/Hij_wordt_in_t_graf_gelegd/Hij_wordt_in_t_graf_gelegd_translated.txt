He is laid in the tomb, Jesus, my Savior; they keep watch in vain, Jesus, my Lord. God’s Son rose from the tomb! He cast off death and its bonds! Through His almighty power, He overcame the power of death, and the last enemy lies vanquished—He rose! He rose! Hallelujah, Jesus lives. They keep watch in vain, Jesus my Savior; the seal is broken by God’s power, Jesus my Lord. When God unlocks the tomb, Jesus my Savior, death loses its prize: Jesus my Lord.
