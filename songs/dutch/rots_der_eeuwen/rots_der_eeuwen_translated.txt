Rock from which life springs forth, shelter me from cruel violence; let the water and the blood that once flowed from Your side be my salvation and my strength against the guilt and power of sin. None of the works of my hands will bring me into the heavenly realm. Neither my possessions nor my wealth meet Your demands; even my tears and sorrow are in vain—You do not save me. Yes, it is You who save me from Your own strict law, from my own foolish guilt, which You blot out in Your patience; God who endured all my evil—Your grace is enough. What I hold in my hands is Your cross for my salvation. You who clothe the naked, who are ready to save, who make lepers clean—clothe me, save me, make me clean. When I leave this life and my breath fails me, when my eye is broken, when my soul has sunk into the barren valley of death, O You, the Rock, be near me!
