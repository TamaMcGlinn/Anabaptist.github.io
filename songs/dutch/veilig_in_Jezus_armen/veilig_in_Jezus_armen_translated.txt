Safe in Jesus’ arms, safe close to Jesus’ heart; there, in His tender hands, my soul finds rest from sorrow. Listen, it is the song of the angels, singing of love and peace, echoing from the halls of heaven, across the glassy sea. Safe in Jesus’ arms, free with my Lord and Savior; free from the turmoil of the world, free from sorrow and worry; free from fear and doubt, free from the power of sin—just a little more suffering, just one more short night. Jesus, my dear Refuge, Jesus, You died for me! May my hope rest eternally on that Rock of the ages! Lord, let me wait patiently until the darkness flees and my eyes behold the glow of Your saving light on those distant shores.
