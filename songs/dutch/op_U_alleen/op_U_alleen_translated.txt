In You alone, my light, my strength, I place my hope. You care for me. Through waves, through storm and night, Your hand guides me. You remain near. Your deep peace, Your great love dispel my fear, drive away death. My steadfast rock, my foundation—You are the ground upon which I stand. You became human; You descended into our pain, guilt, and struggle. You bore the burden, risen Lord who frees us from every curse: You nailed our sins to the cross and bring us home to the Father; for through Your blood, Your life-giving power, we stand free before God. From my first cry to my last breath, I live in You, and You in me. No evil power, no malicious rumor—nothing can separate me from You. For You reign, You conquer, You accept me. I am God’s child. Until You come and call me home for good, You are the purpose of my existence.
