Do not be anxious; your life is more than just food and clothing. Does not He who gave you these things know all your needs? Are you not greater than the grass? How it blooms before it withers! Do not, then, you of little faith, fear that you will be forgotten. See how He clothes the trees and the flowers of the field. He feeds the carefree birds, playing in countless numbers; would He not then clothe you as well, the children He knows, and hear the prayers of those who turn to Him? Seek first the kingdom; your Father will give you all these things in addition. He will not put you to shame, but will show you His goodness. Do not worry about tomorrow, for each day has its own troubles and its own cares; it is God who goes with you.
