Just as you are, and do not rely on your own virtue and holiness. They offer you no security. O guilty sinner, come, O come! Now make the service of God your delight; do not seek your rest in the world; it is Jesus who soothes your sorrow. Weary sinner, come, oh, come! Flee with your sorrow to Golgotha; regard all things as mere filth—you gain more now through God’s grace: Needy sinner, come, oh, come! However beset and burdened, or weighed down by the burden of sin, oh, just cling to the cross: Oh, trembling sinner, come, oh, come! The Spirit and the Bride are calling, “Come!” And those who hear the call, “Come!” And you who have heard it, “Come, yes, come!” The Savior calls: “Come, yes, come!”
