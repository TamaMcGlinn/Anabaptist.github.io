Teach me Your way, O Lord; teach me Your way. Grant me more of Your strength; teach me Your way. Keep me balanced, so that I may walk before You in the full light; teach me Your way. Whatever my circumstances may be, teach me Your way. Whether life is long or short, teach me Your way. When my journey is complete, I fear neither death nor any power, for my soul awaits You; teach me Your way. Whatever this life may bring, He is near. Whether it brings joy or sorrow, He is near. No matter how strong Satan’s power may be, Jesus gives light and strength to everyone who waits for Him; He is near.
