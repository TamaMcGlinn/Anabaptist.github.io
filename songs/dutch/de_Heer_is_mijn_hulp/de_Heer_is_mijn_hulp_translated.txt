Every hour, every moment, I rely on You; Your word alone, O Lord, comforts me now. My soul needs You, every hour, every moment! O bless me, my Savior! To You I come! My soul needs Your help, in joy and in sorrow; Be near me every day, O Lord. Teach me to do Your will, more and more; Fulfill Your Word in my soul, O Lord. My soul ever longs for You, O Holy God! I am Yours—what a blessed fate!
