Glory be to You, risen Lord, victory be to You, all honor be to You! You endured all human suffering to set us free into a new existence: May light shine in the darkness, may new peace descend where there is no longer any hope. Grant us, then, to live in the new light; give us the word that brings peace here: Glory to You, risen Lord, victory to You, now and forevermore. From a shining stream, an angel descended, and removed the stone from the conquered tomb. Behold Him appearing, Jesus our Lord! He brings all His own back into His arms. Be, then, the Lord’s people, joyful and well-disposed, and say again and again: Christ conquers! Should I still fear, now that He lives forever—He who has healed me, He who gives me peace? In His divine being my glory is great; I have nothing to fear in life or in death! 

To You be the glory, dear Lord, to You be the praise, to You be all honor! Chorus:
