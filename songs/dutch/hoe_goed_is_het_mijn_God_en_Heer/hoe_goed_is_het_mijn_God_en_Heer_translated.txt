How good it is, my God and Lord, to sing Your praise forever. The day proclaims Your great love. The night sings of Your faithfulness in times of need. How good the day of rest is to me: Then I sing myself free from worries. When, following David’s example, I reverently praise God, my Creator. My heart rejoices in the Lord. His Word tells it time and again. What He once prepared for me will never be put to shame throughout eternity.
