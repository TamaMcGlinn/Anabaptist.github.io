1. Hear us! We lift up our hands; come to us, accept our offerings, with which we stand before You in gratitude. 2. Your eye beholds us here with our treasures. Do You, whom even the heavens cannot contain, dwell in a house built by us? 3. You see and hear what our mouths seek to say; it is a stammering, inadequate sign, a weak and powerless human word. 4. In silent awe we have come before You. Turn then Your ear to our supplications; watch over us with Your eyes night and day! 5. You speak to us; You have chosen humankind: in this place You make Your words heard; here You are, for here Your name dwells. 6. O, answer us when we pray together; descend from Your heights into our midst! In Your forgiveness we dwell.
